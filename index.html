Workflow

How I usually build a model

Reference & Concept

I gather additional references, establish a color palette that fits the model’s theme, and create a clear concept before starting the build.

3D Modeling

I build the model in Blockbench, keeping the structure, folders, and UVs organized to make the texturing process clean and efficient.

Texturing & Optimization

I texture each model with a vanilla-inspired approach, paying attention to every face and detail. When necessary, I optimize the final asset to keep it lightweight and efficient in-game.
