Workflow

How I usually build an add-on

Reference & Concept

I gather extra references beyond the ones provided, work out a color palette that fits the model's theme, and sketch before starting the build.

3D Modeling

I start the 3D modeling, keeping folders and UVs well organized to avoid issues later during texturing.

Texturing & Optimization

I texture following a vanilla style, cube by cube, leaving no empty faces. If needed, I finish by optimizing the texture to keep it lightweight.
